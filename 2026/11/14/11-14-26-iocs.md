## Coverage

All six historical structured files in this repository were retrieved completely, their bytes checked against GitHub blob identities and sizes, and all CSV/JSON rows compared: 238 records plus six documented alternate hashes. No previous-repository data was fetched or used. NEW means absent from this checked archive as an exact canonical IOC, not a newly observed campaign or newly active malware.

Typed source-level decisions recorded 132 historical duplicate observations representing 131 distinct IOCs, and 2623 rejected candidate observations. Of those rejections, 2,546 were a generic ARTEX console inventory whose membership alone does not establish maliciousness. Other rejected observations include shared/proxy origins, uncertain direction or tenant scope, expired infrastructure, legitimate tools, media and in-memory hashes without a file-telemetry mapping. Counts describe assessed evidence observations, not unique threats or every technical string in the reviewed reports.

Arctic Wolf’s high-confidence domain heading says 98, while its visually checked table lists 99 distinct domains. One explicitly lapsed domain was omitted; 98 entries from that table and 14 additional specifically documented hosts are retained. Only malicious files are included; ordinary lure media, expired-link support pages, operator metadata and personalized session paths are excluded. This one investigation supplies 122 of 213 indicators; broader second-pass research did not justify padding other families.

Access gaps: URLhaus, ThreatFox and MalwareBazaar record evidence remained behind verification gates; the MyCERT lead could not be inspected. Unsupported candidates from those checks were omitted. Canonical web retrieval recovered original Sophos, SentinelOne and Datadog reports after direct access failures. Older featured reports and re-posted news dates were not treated as fresh original research. This is a coverage-qualified result, not evidence of a quiet threat day.

Sources for most accepted values are single primary investigations. Passive corroboration attempts did not establish independent confirmation for most network indicators. The FBI ARTEX advisory and Microsoft’s older original evidence are linked, not independent. Related campaign studies and alternate hashes do not increase novel-threat counts.

Both hunt queries have a 30-day lookback and a final 500-result cap after filtering. All IPs are reviewed destinations. Hosts and full URLs match exactly; child subdomains are not automatically included. Encrypted traffic, Tor routing, missing full URL fields and incomplete hash telemetry can hide matches. Microsoft Defender SHA-256 fields may be unpopulated; SHA-1 alternatives are included only where documented. Older underlying activity may fall outside the hunt window. Queries received static type, escaping and set-agreement checks; they were not executed on live Microsoft Defender or Elastic systems.
