## Coverage

The primary window begins at 2026-11-21T13:57:11Z and the extended window at 2026-11-16T13:57:11Z. Date-only sources are interpreted conservatively. Publication, observation and collection dates are recorded separately. Unknown observations remain unknown. No current server activity is inferred from publication dates or a running process.

Research is partial. Some original evidence and contextual public records could not be inspected or dated well enough; alternate routes recovered a portion. These gaps limit freshness and independent corroboration. This is not a finding of a quiet threat day. Same-publisher appendices and repeated news coverage do not establish independent confirmation.

All accepted indicators have directly reviewed exact-value evidence. Shared CDN IPs, provider roots, legitimate binaries, unresolved dates, relative paths, operator-only tools and insufficiently scoped infrastructure were excluded. Original values and URL case are retained in JSON/CSV and both hunts; the table below is defanged. No malware samples were downloaded, and no malicious infrastructure was contacted.

The 30-day hunts filter before a final 500-result cap. Defender requires network, file and process tables; Linux router telemetry may not be available there. Elastic requires compatible ECS mappings and full URL telemetry. Extracted, encrypted or memory-only component hashes may need separate content visibility. Exact matches are investigative leads, and neither hunt proves compromise. Queries received static validation only and were not executed on live platforms.
